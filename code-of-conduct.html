Worker Code of Conduct

Worker Code of Conduct – Mandatory

All workers associated with Shrimant Multi Facilities & Services 79 are required to follow this Code of Conduct while applying for, accepting, or performing any assigned work.

Failure to comply may result in immediate action without prior notice.

1. Professional Behavior

Workers must behave in a professional, respectful, and disciplined manner at all times.

  • Respect must be shown towards clients, fellow workers, supervisors, and company representatives.
  • Abusive language, arguments, harassment, threats, or inappropriate behavior will not be tolerated.
  • Workers must follow instructions related to work scope, timing, safety, and conduct.

2. Prohibited Activities (Strict Policy)

The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Consumption or possession of alcohol
  • Tobacco, cigarettes, gutkha, pan, or similar substances
  • Drugs or any illegal or intoxicating substances

This prohibition applies:

  • During working hours
  • At the work location
  • While traveling for assigned work
  • While representing the company in any manner

Reporting to work under the influence of any such substances is also strictly forbidden.

3. Workplace Discipline

  • Workers must report on time as per assigned schedule.
  • Leaving the work location without permission is not allowed.
  • Workers must maintain cleanliness, safety, and discipline at the workplace.
  • Any damage caused due to negligence or misconduct may result in liability.

4. Violation & Action

If a worker violates this Code of Conduct:

  • Immediate removal from the work site
  • Cancellation of the assigned work
  • Suspension or permanent removal from future assignments
  • Payment may be withheld or adjusted
  • Legal action may be initiated if required

Ignorance of rules will not be accepted as an excuse.
