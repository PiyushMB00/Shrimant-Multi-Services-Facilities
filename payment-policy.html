Payment Policy

Worker Payment Terms

This Payment Policy explains how and when payments are processed for workers associated with Shrimant Multi Facilities & Services 79.

1. Payment Condition

  • Payment is released only after successful completion of assigned work.
  • Client confirmation or satisfaction is mandatory for payment processing.
  • Partial or incomplete work may result in delayed or reduced payment.

2. Payment Timeline

  • Payments are generally processed within 24 to 72 working hours after work completion and confirmation.
  • Delays may occur in case of disputes, complaints, or verification issues.

3. Mode of Payment

Payment is made via:

  • Bank transfer
  • UPI or other approved digital methods

Cash payments are not guaranteed.

4. Deductions & Penalties

Payment may be:

  • Adjusted for incomplete work
  • Deducted in case of damages caused
  • Withheld due to misconduct, policy violations, or client complaints

Any platform or service fee, if applicable, will be communicated in advance.

5. No Advance Guarantee

  • Advance payment is not guaranteed unless explicitly approved.
  • The company is not responsible for personal expenses incurred by the worker unless agreed in writing.

6. Dispute Resolution

  • Payment disputes must be reported within a reasonable time.
  • The company’s decision after review will be final.
