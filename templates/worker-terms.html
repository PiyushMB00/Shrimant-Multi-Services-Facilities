Worker Terms

Contract-Based Engagement Terms

These Worker Terms govern the engagement between workers and Shrimant Multi Facilities & Services 79.

By registering or accepting work, the worker agrees to all terms stated below.

1. Nature of Engagement

  • Workers are engaged strictly on a contractual and task-based basis.
  • This is not permanent employment.
  • No assurance of regular work, salary, or long-term engagement is provided.
  • Each work assignment is independent and limited to its defined scope and duration.

2. Role of the Company

  • The company acts as a service facilitator between clients and workers.
  • The company does not guarantee continuous work or income.
  • Assignment of work depends on client requirements, availability, and worker suitability.

3. Acceptance of Work

  • Workers may accept or decline assignments.
  • Once accepted, the worker is obligated to complete the work professionally.
  • Failure to report or complete accepted work without valid reason may result in penalties or suspension.

4. Documents & Verification

  • Workers must provide accurate personal and identification details.
  • Documents may be verified for authenticity.
  • Submission of false or misleading information may lead to immediate removal.

5. Termination of Association

The company reserves the right to:

  • Suspend or remove any worker without prior notice
  • Cancel future assignments due to misconduct, complaints, or policy violations

Workers may stop accepting work at any time but must complete already accepted assignments.

6. Liability

  • Workers are responsible for their actions during work.
  • Any loss, damage, or legal issue caused by worker misconduct will be the worker’s responsibility.
  • The company shall not be liable for disputes arising due to worker negligence or rule violations.
